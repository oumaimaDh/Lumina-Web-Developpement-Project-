Everything you need to know about joining our community

Getting started is super easy! Just browse our initiatives, find one that speaks to you, and click "Join Initiative." You'll be connected with the organizers and other volunteers right away. No complicated sign-up process!

Absolutely! Lumina is 100% free for everyone. We believe social impact should be accessible to all, so we'll never charge you to discover, join, or create initiatives. Our mission is to connect communities, not profit from them.
